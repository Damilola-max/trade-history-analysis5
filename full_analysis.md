## 3. Methodology
1. **Copy Trading:**
   - Replicated trades based on top traders' behavior.
   - Observed for a fixed period of three months.

2. **Hedging:**
   - Analyzed the reduction of downside risk using correlated assets.
   - Tested various hedging ratios and rebalancing intervals.

3. **Combined Approaches:**
   - Strategies tested independently and with joint optimization.

### Metrics Used
- Net Profit / Loss
- Sharpe Ratio
- Maximum Drawdown
- Hedging Effectiveness Ratio
